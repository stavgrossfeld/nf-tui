# The nf-tui name

The code is MIT. Take it, change it, ship it, sell it.

The name isn't part of that. **If you distribute a fork, call it something
else.** That's the whole ask.

Say it's "based on nf-tui" as loudly as you like — that's accurate and useful
to your users.

**Fine, no need to ask:** packaging it (conda, Homebrew, Spack, your cluster's
module system), writing about it, screenshots, saying your tool works with it.

**Ask first:** naming your fork or product `nf-tui` or something close enough
to be confused with it, or implying this project endorses yours.

To ask, open an issue: <https://github.com/stavgrossfeld/nf-tui/issues>
